# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and the people served by nonprofits with respect. Discuss
technical disagreements constructively and use privacy-safe examples.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing
someone's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content
or restrict participation to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, `@flemingw`, through an
existing private communication channel. Establish a public reporting route
before opening community contributions.
